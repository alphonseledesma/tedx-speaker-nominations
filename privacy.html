Privacy policy

How we use what you share when you nominate a speaker. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who we are

This form is run by the organizing team of TEDxAteneodeManilaU, an independently organized TEDx event, to collect speaker nominations from the public.

What we collect

  • From your Google account: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your password, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • From the form: the answers you type about the person you nominate, including their name, optional sex/gender and age, occupation, your reasons for nominating them, and their contact details.

How we use it

  • Your email confirms that each nomination comes from a real person and lets us send you a copy of your answers.
  • The nomination details are used only to review potential speakers and to contact the people nominated.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Nominations are saved in a Google Sheet owned by the organizing team. Only members of the speaker committee can open it. We do not share nominations outside the organizing team.

Drafts on your device

While you fill in the form, your unsent answers are saved as a draft so you don't lose them: in your own browser, and with your email in the organizing team's Google Sheet so you can continue on another device. Only the organizing team can see drafts, and yours is deleted as soon as you submit.

How long we keep it
